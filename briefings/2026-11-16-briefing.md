## 1. SLOVENIA

Yesterday's first round of local elections dominates everything.

**Ljubljana goes to a runoff for the first time in 20 years.** At 99.23% counted,
Zoran Janković took 34.97% and Levica's Luka Mesec 26.16% — Janković had won every
Ljubljana mayoral race outright since 2002. Runoff is 29 November. Janković: "Welcome
to the runoff, Luka Mesec"; Mesec says he's confident of winning.

**45 of 212 municipalities head to a second round.** Also unresolved: Maribor, Koper
(Bržan 47% vs Popovič 31%), Kranj (incumbent Rakovec eliminated — the night's biggest
upset), Nova Gorica and Ptuj. Celje, Novo mesto and Velenje kept their mayors.
Turnout was up — roughly 10 percentage points higher than 2022 in Ljubljana.

**SDS claimed the party scoreboard — but which scoreboard depends on the outlet.**
RTV SLO/STA: SDS was the most successful parliamentary party by mayors elected
(10 of 66 candidates; SD 8, SLS 6, NSi 5, Gibanje Svoboda just 1), and ~574
proportional council seats.

> ⚠️ **Framing disagreement.** The numbers largely agree; the verdict doesn't.
> - **RTV SLO / STA** — neutral tally, "most successful *among parliamentary parties*."
> - **Portal24** — "SDS strongest, Svoboda badly lagging," arguing Svoboda has failed
>   to build a local network four years on.
> - **Svet24** — headline pun "SD(S)", and different mayor counts (SDS 16, NSi 12,
>   SLS 11), probably counting supported/independent candidates rather than party
>   tickets. Worth not treating as contradicting RTV.
> - **Večer** — the counter-framing: independents and voter-group lists actually won
>   the council vote (31%), ahead of SDS (15.9%) and Svoboda (15.6%). On that read the
>   "party winner" story understates the real story, which is non-party lists.

**Smaller items.** STA (via Avto-magazin): fuel prices drop from Tuesday — ~€1.445/l
for 95-octane, €1.80/l for diesel. Winter tyre/equipment requirement has been in force
since 15 November, running to 15 March.

## 2. GLOBAL

**Strait of Hormuz: Iran says six tankers have hit mines since Sunday.** The IRGC
reports six vessels on fire, Iranian state media (ISNA) says one supertanker carrying
2m+ barrels sank, and Iran has warned shipowners that the strait's southern route is
mined — saying the tankers used an "unauthorised" route. A third US carrier, the USS
Theodore Roosevelt, has reportedly arrived in the region.

> ⚠️ **Wire vs. other framing.** Reuters and AP attribute all of it strictly to the
> IRGC and say they could not independently verify the reports; CENTCOM hasn't
> confirmed the carrier timing. Iranian state media and several regional outlets
> report the sinkings as established fact. Tally also drifts by outlet (two → three →
> six tankers). Treat the count as claimed, not confirmed.

**Bahrain intercepts Iranian strikes on a US base.** The IRGC says it sent six drones
at a US radar installation at the Al-Riffa heliport, calling it a "decisive and
crushing response." Bahrain says its air defences intercepted the attacks before
impact; CENTCOM has confirmed no damage or casualties. Trump posted on Truth Social
that "we don't need a deal with Iran," while Tehran's conditions for talks include
sanctions relief and an end to the naval blockade.

**Zelensky says the US floated Donbas-for-NATO.** After the Abu Dhabi talks, envoys
Witkoff and Kushner reportedly asked whether Ukraine would withdraw from Donbas if it
joined NATO — the first time Zelensky says that linkage has been raised. He rejected
ceding territory, and said any deal would go to a referendum only with firm security
guarantees, army funding and reconstruction money.

> ⚠️ **Framing differs.** Zelensky's own quote is specific to Donbas; Time's headline
> generalises it to "withdrawing its forces." Bloomberg leads instead on "Kushner
> leans on Europe," foregrounding the pressure on European capitals rather than the

**Gaza: winter storm floods 70,000 tents.** An estimated 350,000 people were affected

equipment until Hamas fully disarms. The dispute is over how much is getting in, not
whether shelter is short. Separately, Israel killed a senior Hamas commander as the
ceasefire stagnates; the Palestinian health ministry counts 1,600+ killed in strikes

## 3. SCIENCE & HEALTH

**✅ CONFIRMED (peer-reviewed).** *Nature*, 6 Nov: in vivo CRISPR base editing of
*ANGPTL3* cut the target protein by >90% in monkeys, durable to **three years after a
single dose**, with LDL down 40–50% and triglycerides down 50–70% in mouse and primate
models. A genuinely striking durability result.
*⚠️ The human work is early:* VERVE-201 is only in phase 1b. A separate CRISPR
Therapeutics trial (CTX310) reported a 63% triglyceride drop by day 30 — but in just
10 people and company-reported. **Preliminary.**

**✅ CONFIRMED — FDA rejection (15 Nov).** The FDA issued a complete response letter
for Summit/Akeso's ivonescimab in non-small-cell lung cancer: the phase 3 HARMONi
trial slowed progression but missed overall survival (HR 0.79, p=0.057). Akeso shares
fell as much as 32%. This derails the first US approval for the much-hyped PD-1xVEGF
class.

**✅ CONFIRMED — FDA approval (9 Nov, via STAT).** Bayer's Sunharbo approved for
stroke recurrence — first in a new anticoagulant class designed to prevent clots
without raising bleeding risk. Phase 3: secondary strokes 6.2% vs 8.4% on placebo.

**✅ CONFIRMED — important negative result (AHA, 7 Nov, via STAT).** Novartis's
pelacarsen lowered Lp(a) substantially in the HORIZON trial but did **not** reduce
heart attacks, strokes or deaths versus placebo — a real setback for the Lp(a)
causal hypothesis.

**🔶 CANDIDATE, not confirmed.** The Vera C. Rubin Observatory has flagged 2026 ED138,
~46 AU out, as a possible sixth dwarf planet — the biggest such find in ~20 years. Size
estimates range widely (600–1,500 km diameter). It hid because its orbit sits near the
Milky Way's plane. Needs infrared follow-up to pin down mass, density and shape before
any official classification.

**🔶 UNVERIFIED.** A class-wide FDA label warning for NAION (a rare eye condition)
across GLP-1 drugs appears only in a secondary recap, not FDA materials. Flagging it
as unconfirmed rather than reporting it.
