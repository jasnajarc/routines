## 1. What science matters

**BICAN basal ganglia atlas — the week's real event.** 28 coordinated papers, 12 Nov, across *Cell* and sister journals: a cross-species cell atlas (human/macaque/marmoset/mouse), 55 institutions, 9 countries. What matters is the **consensus cell taxonomy** — a shared naming system, long the field's bottleneck. Data open. Cell counts disagree by source (17.4M on the BICAN portal vs 25.5M in secondary coverage). [BICAN/Allen Institute, 12 Nov]

**FDA confirms insertional oncogenesis with Zolgensma.** Postmarketing reports showed vector DNA integrated at the *GLI1* oncogene in tumour tissue — first product-attributable demonstration of a risk the US label called theoretical. FDA still judges benefit > risk; applies to both onasemnogene products (shared vector). [STAT 9 Nov; NeurologyLive]

**Contrarian read on AI discovery.** *Nature Reviews Drug Discovery*'s November perspective (Bender, Thomas, Cortés-Ciriano) argues AI has delivered limited **clinical** impact to date. Also new: kinase landscape review (13 Nov), histone lysine demethylases (11 Nov). *(dates unconfirmed)*

## 2. What industry buys

- **Discovery platforms, not late-stage assets.** argenx/Unnatural Products (9 Nov, $550M upfront, to $1.145B), macrocyclic peptides; AstraZeneca/Omniose (preclinical); Merck KGaA/Silence ~$1.1B EV (early Nov).
- **Structured finance over balance-sheet risk.** Blackstone behind ~$1.5B of Pfizer's Metsera obesity launch (12 Nov). Charles River paid $470M for two Frontage labs.
- **Modality rotation is cutting jobs.** Cellectis cutting 70% of staff to pivot allogeneic CAR-T → in vivo (12 Nov); Vertex cut 78 at Crinetics post-$10B deal.
- **Regulatory:** Imfinzi approved in muscle-invasive bladder cancer; Bayer's Sunharbo approved for stroke recurrence (9 Nov; 6.2% vs 8.4% placebo). FDA **rejected** Summit's PD-1xVEGF ivonescimab in NSCLC (15 Nov) — reads across the bispecific class.
- **MFN is reshaping launch sequencing.** Drugmakers weighing delayed or withdrawn ex-US launches to protect US reference prices (one reportedly eyeing pulling a product from France); EU rare-disease firms hesitant to enter the US. 26 deals cover ~90% of the branded US market. [STAT 11 Nov; Law360/Goodwin]
- **AI ROI data stays thin.** No new McKinsey/Deloitte/BCG *pharma R&D* AI report this week. New: McKinsey "Future of healthcare" (9 Nov); BCG consumerization survey, n=10,000 (12 Nov). Best standing figures remain Deloitte's 22% scaled AI / 9% significant returns.

## 3. What Europe funds

- **FP10 fight escalated.** 13 Nov: 72 Nobel laureates signed an open letter demanding ≥€200bn. Positions: Commission €175bn, Parliament ≥€200bn, evaluation experts €220bn, Irish Presidency ~€158bn. Horizon/European Competitiveness Fund governance unresolved (€68bn would sit inside the ECF). Adoption targeted before summer 2027, for a 1 Jan 2028 start.
- **EIF money into late-stage biotech.** Sofinnova closed Crossover II at €725M (16 Nov), backed by the European Tech Champions Initiative via the EIF, for ~15 late-stage European biotech/medtech — the clearest EU answer yet to the scale-up-capital critique.
- **Nothing new from EFPIA or DG R&I in window** (hosts blocked; no indexed items). Standing context: Horizon Europe health-call budgets cut 25% (2026) and 14% (2027) in work-programme drafts.

## 4. What's new in neuroscience/neurology

- **Mechanism:** human single-neuron recordings implicate the **claustrum** in tracking uncertainty and prediction error [*Nature Neuroscience*, 10 Nov]. GWAS of multidimensional pain, >1.2M people, **343 loci** *(date unconfirmed)*.
- **Worth reading:** *Nature Reviews Neuroscience* on the **neuroscience of delirium** (13 Nov) — prevalent, under-studied, strongly tied to dementia, commercially under-served.
- **Clinical/commercial:** the Zolgensma finding hits SMA gene therapy directly and AAV neuro programmes generally. Regeneron posted positive Phase 2 POTS data, pivotal planned. **No Alzheimer's readouts in window.** Biogen's two Phase 3 litifilimab lupus readouts land in H1 December. SfN opens 18 Nov.

**So what for a consulting engagement.** (1) Zolgensma is immediate risk-reframing work for any client with AAV in the pipeline — label language, long-term follow-up, payer conversations. (2) MFN is now a launch-sequencing and portfolio-geography problem, not a pricing one: clients need a market-by-market withdraw/delay/enter model. (3) The NRDD "limited clinical impact" line plus Deloitte's 9%-returns figure are the credible sceptic's brief for any AI-in-R&D business case.
